## Final image prompt

Use case: style-transfer. Image 1 is an existing background to REDRAW, not merely upscale or sharpen. Image 2 is the precise pixel-art STYLE REFERENCE for a coherent game. The user rejects the muddy painted-looking terrain next to the crisp sprites. Produce a HIGH RESOLUTION 3072 x 1728 landscape map plate with genuinely crisp tile-art edges and small uniform pixel marks, NOT big blurred blotches. Retain the broad quarter-view ruined courtyard composition, brazier in upper left, ruined walls and roots at the outer perimeter, wide open center and clear east-west passage. Redraw surfaces using disciplined handcrafted pixel tiles: individual compact rectangular stone shapes, thin clean dark one-pixel-like edge lines, small stepped corners, restrained single-pixel edge highlights and exactly three solid shading values per material. Ground must be quieter than characters but have SHARP CONTOURS: low contrast comes from a narrow color ramp, never blur, fog, smearing, watercolor shading, chunky soft blobs, oversized pixels or pixelation filters. Flat clean dirt patches alternate with compact outlined paving groups; no pepper-noise texture. Keep paving clusters moderately small compared to the walls so a 48-pixel-tall creature in the game would occupy around 2-3 paving stones in height. Do not add central obstacles, high props, stairs or gates. Border walls use crisp flat faces with distinct top/side shade bands, moss as a few tiny sharp clusters. Slate gray and muted earth/olive colors matching image 2, with enough edge contrast to look as crisp as the beasts. Avoid all painted gradients and simulated low resolution impressionism. Small warm flame accent only; no depth of field, bloom, haze or lens effects. Full-bleed opaque background map, no text, HUD, characters, frames, letters, grids or watermarks.
